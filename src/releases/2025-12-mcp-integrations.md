date: 2025-12-19

Interloom now supports the Model Context Protocol (MCP), an open standard for connecting AI assistants to external tools and services. This means your organization can extend the AI agent's capabilities with custom integrations — connecting it to internal APIs, proprietary databases, or third-party services specific to your industry.

MCP turns Interloom into an extensible platform where the agent's toolset grows with your needs, rather than being limited to what ships out of the box. If you have a system your team relies on daily, it can now become part of the agent's toolkit.
