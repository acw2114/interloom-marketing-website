Interloom now supports the Model Context Protocol (MCP), an open standard for connecting AI assistants to external tools and services. Extend the agent's capabilities with custom integrations — internal APIs, proprietary databases, or third-party services specific to your industry.

If your team relies on a system daily, it can now become part of the agent's toolkit.
