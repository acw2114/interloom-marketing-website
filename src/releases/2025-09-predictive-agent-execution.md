title: Predictive Next Steps and Plan Execution
date: 2025-09-15
tag: AX
---

The assistant is now more proactive about keeping work moving forward. After completing an action, it analyzes the current state of your task and suggests the most relevant next steps — surfacing follow-ups you might not have considered and recommending the right specialist agent for each one.

On top of that, agents can now apply structured plans directly to your work. Instead of just suggesting what to do, the assistant can take a high-level plan and execute it step by step — turning strategy into action with a single interaction. Together, these changes shift the AI from a reactive tool to a genuine collaborator that anticipates and drives your workflow.
