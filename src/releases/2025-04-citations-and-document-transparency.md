---
title: Citations and Document Transparency
date: 2025-04-25
tag: Tools
---

When the assistant draws on documents in your workspace, every reference is now traceable back to its source. Responses include inline citations — clickable references embedded directly in the text that link to the specific passage the agent used. Each claim or data point is annotated with a reference to the exact chunk of the original document. Click a citation and you go straight to the source material.

This creates an auditable chain from answer to evidence: you can verify the agent's reasoning, trace a conclusion back to its origin, and build confidence in AI-generated responses — especially in workflows where accuracy matters.

Every file now also shows its indexing status — processed and searchable, currently being indexed, or pending — so you always know exactly when a document becomes part of the agent's knowledge base. No more guessing whether the AI has access to a file or where it got its information from.
