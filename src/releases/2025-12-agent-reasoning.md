title: Agent Reasoning and Reliability
date: 2025-12-22
tag: AX
---

When the assistant works through a complex request, you can now see its thinking process in real time. A "thinking" step shows how the agent analyzes the problem, considers approaches, and decides which tools to use. Follow its logic, catch misunderstandings early, and course-correct mid-conversation.

The agent is also significantly more reliable at multi-step work. Improved turn completion means it can execute multiple tools in sequence, chain results together, and exit cleanly once the task is fully resolved — instead of stopping prematurely or requiring manual nudges between steps.
