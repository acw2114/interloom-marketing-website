title: Agent Reasoning and Reliability
date: 2025-12-22
tag: AX
---

When your AI assistant works through a complex request, you can now see its thinking process in real time. A new "thinking" step appears in the conversation, showing how the agent analyzes the problem, considers different approaches, and decides which tools to use.

Instead of receiving answers from a black box, you can follow the agent's logic, catch misunderstandings early, and feel confident that the response is grounded in sound reasoning. It also makes it easier to course-correct mid-conversation by understanding exactly where the agent's thinking diverged from your expectations.

Beyond transparency, the agent is now significantly more reliable at complex, multi-step work. Improved turn completion logic means the agent knows when it's actually done — it can execute multiple tools in sequence, chain the results together, and exit cleanly once the task is fully resolved, rather than stopping prematurely or continuing unnecessarily.

For requests that require the agent to search a document, cross-reference it with case details, draft a response, and update a record, the assistant now handles the entire sequence as a coherent workflow instead of requiring manual nudges between steps.
