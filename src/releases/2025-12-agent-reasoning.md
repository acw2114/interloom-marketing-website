title: Agent Reasoning and Reliability
date: 2025-12-22
tag: AX
---

When the assistant works through a complex request, you can now see its thinking process in real time. A new "thinking" step appears in the conversation, showing how the agent analyzes the problem, considers different approaches, and decides which tools to use.

Instead of receiving answers from a black box, you can follow the agent's logic, catch misunderstandings early, and feel confident the response is grounded in sound reasoning. It also makes it easier to course-correct mid-conversation by understanding exactly where the agent's thinking diverged from your expectations.

Beyond transparency, the agent is now significantly more reliable at complex, multi-step work. Improved turn completion logic means it knows when it's actually done — it can execute multiple tools in sequence, chain results together, and exit cleanly once the task is fully resolved, rather than stopping prematurely or continuing unnecessarily. For requests that require searching a document, cross-referencing it with case details, drafting a response, and updating a record, the assistant handles the entire sequence as a coherent workflow instead of requiring manual nudges between steps.
