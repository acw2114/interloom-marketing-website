title: Rebuilt Rich Text Editor
date: 2025-09-05
tag: UX
---

We've completely rebuilt the text editing experience across Interloom from the ground up. The new editor, powered by a modern Tiptap-based architecture, delivers a faster, more reliable writing experience for notes, task descriptions, and messages.

Formatting is more intuitive, link handling works as you'd expect, and the editor handles complex content — including entity references and inline mentions — without the quirks and inconsistencies of the previous version.

If you spend your day writing and editing within Interloom, this is the kind of foundational upgrade that makes everything feel sharper.
