date: 2025-06-30
tag: UX
---

Open files directly inside Interloom — PDFs with page navigation, images with inline preview, email files with formatted rendering, and notes with attachments. Open any file from a thread, a task's files panel, or search results and it appears in a side panel alongside your current work.

No more downloading files or switching apps to review a contract or check an attachment.
