title: SharePoint Integration
date: 2025-12-09
tag: Tools
---

Your AI assistant can now search across your organization's SharePoint document libraries directly from within Interloom. When working through a case, the agent can locate relevant policies, templates, contracts, or reference materials stored in SharePoint without you leaving the platform or switching tabs.

This bridges the gap between your document management system and your active casework, so the information you need is always within reach of your AI-assisted workflow.
