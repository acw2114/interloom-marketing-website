---
title: Procedure Stages
date: 2025-11-25
tag: AX
---

Procedures can now be broken down into sequential stages. Define the stages a case should move through — from intake to review to resolution — and track progress as work advances through each phase.

Stages give teams a shared understanding of where things stand and what comes next, replacing ad-hoc status updates with a clear, visual progression. Especially useful for standardized processes like onboarding, claims processing, or compliance reviews, where knowing exactly where a case sits in the pipeline is critical.
