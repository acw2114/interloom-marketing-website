title: Procedure Stages
date: 2025-11-25
tag: AX
---

Procedures can now be broken down into sequential stages, bringing structured process management to your workflows. Define the stages a case or task should move through — from intake to review to resolution — and track progress as work advances through each phase.

Stages give teams a shared understanding of where things stand and what comes next, replacing ad-hoc status updates with a clear, visual progression.

This is especially powerful for teams running standardized processes like onboarding, claims processing, or compliance reviews, where knowing exactly where a case sits in the pipeline is critical.
