We've added a flexible tagging system for organizing work. Apply custom tags to any task, then filter, search, and group by the categories that matter to your team — priority levels, client names, departments, or custom categories. Tags appear inline on task cards and in list views, making it easy to spot patterns and quickly locate related work.

A dedicated tag picker makes adding and managing tags effortless: start typing and matching tags surface instantly. The assistant can also suggest and apply tags automatically based on task content, so your organizational system actually keeps up with the pace of incoming work.
