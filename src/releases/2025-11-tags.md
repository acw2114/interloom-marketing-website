Interloom now supports a flexible tagging system for organizing your work. Apply custom tags to any task, then filter, search, and group by the categories that matter to your team — whether that's priority levels, client names, departments, or custom categories.

Tags appear inline on task cards and in list views, making it easy to spot patterns and quickly locate related work across your workspace.

A dedicated Tag Picker makes it effortless to add and manage tags: start typing and matching tags surface instantly. Your AI assistant can also suggest and apply tags automatically based on task content, ensuring consistent categorization without manual effort.

For teams that rely on tags to organize high-volume workloads, the combination of fast manual tagging, intelligent auto-tagging, and tag-based filtering means your organizational system actually keeps up with the pace of incoming work rather than falling behind it.
