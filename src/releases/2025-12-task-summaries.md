---
title: Automatic Task Summaries
date: 2025-12-01
tag: AX
---

Tasks now receive automatically generated summaries that capture the key points from every conversation thread. As discussions unfold — across messages, emails, and agent interactions — Interloom distills them into a concise summary that lives at the top of the task.

For anyone picking up a case they weren't part of, or revisiting a task after days away, summaries eliminate the need to scroll through entire conversation histories. You get the full picture in seconds.
