---
title: Global Search
date: 2025-04-09
tag: UX
---

A new platform-wide search makes it possible to find any task across your workspace instantly. Type a query and we search across task titles, descriptions, and content — returning ranked results with highlighted matches so you can scan and locate what you need in seconds.

Search is accessible from the nav bar or via a keyboard shortcut, and results open directly in the panel view so you never lose your place. Behind the scenes, it's powered by precomputed vectors and a dedicated search service, so results come back fast even at scale.
