Find any task across your workspace instantly. A new platform-wide search covers task titles, descriptions, and content — returning ranked results with highlighted matches.

Accessible from the nav bar or a keyboard shortcut. Results open in the panel view so you don't lose your place. Powered by precomputed vectors, so results come back fast even at scale.
