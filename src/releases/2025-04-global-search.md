---
title: Global Search
date: 2025-04-09
tag: UX
---

A new platform-wide search makes it possible to find any task across your workspace instantly. Type a query and Interloom searches across task titles, descriptions, and content — returning ranked results with highlighted matches so you can scan and locate what you need in seconds.

Search is accessible from the navigation bar or via a global keyboard shortcut, and results open directly in the panel view so you never lose your place.

For teams managing hundreds or thousands of active cases, this replaces scrolling and filtering with a fast, direct path to any piece of work. Behind the scenes, search is powered by precomputed vectors and a dedicated search service, so results come back fast even at scale.
