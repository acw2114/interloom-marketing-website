Full email lifecycle inside the platform. The assistant composes drafts informed by the full case context — prior correspondence, documents, conversation history — and you send directly from Interloom. Replies route back to the correct task thread automatically using cryptographically signed reply-to addresses.

This closes the loop: inbound mail creates tasks, the agent drafts responses, you review and send, replies land back in the same thread.
