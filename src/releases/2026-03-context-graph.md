We're building a context graph that connects all Interloom primitives — Spaces, Tasks, Notes, Files, Agents, Procedures, Stages, and Subtasks — through explicit ownership and reference relationships.

This is the foundation for the Interloom Assistant. It will traverse these relationships to surface relevant context, explain dependencies, and help you make decisions with more confidence and less digging.
