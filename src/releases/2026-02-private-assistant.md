![Private assistant](/images/releases/private-assistant.png)

You can now start private conversations with the assistant outside of any task or case. Use it for quick research, brainstorming, exploratory questions, or any interaction that doesn't belong in a specific case thread.

Private chats live in their own section of the nav, keep their own conversation history, and won't clutter your case data. Think of it as having the full power of the assistant available for ad-hoc work — a scratchpad with intelligence.
