Stages bring structured, sequential process management to Interloom. Define the phases a case should move through — intake, investigation, review, resolution — and the platform tracks progression automatically. Stages start the moment a first message arrives, so cases begin advancing through your workflow without manual intervention.

From the task overview, managers can see at a glance which stage every active case sits in across the entire pipeline.

Teams have full control to create, edit, reorder, and remove stages within any procedure, adapting processes as they evolve rather than locking them in at design time.

For organizations running standardized workflows — onboarding, compliance reviews, claims processing, support escalation — stages replace informal status tracking with a visible, enforceable structure that ensures nothing falls through the cracks and every case follows the same path.
