---
title: Workspace Navigation and Files
date: 2025-11-11
tag: UX
---

Spaces now appear directly in the sidebar, giving you one-click access to any workspace without drilling through menus. Each space also gets a dedicated Files tab — a single place to browse, preview, and manage every document, attachment, and file associated with that workspace.

For teams managing dozens of spaces with hundreds of files, this eliminates the friction of context-switching and makes everything feel immediately navigable. Finding the right document takes one click instead of three.
