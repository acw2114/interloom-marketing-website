---
title: Workspace Navigation and File Management
date: 2025-11-11
tag: UX
---

Spaces now appear directly in the sidebar navigation, giving you one-click access to any workspace without drilling through menus or searching. Each space also gets a dedicated Files tab — a single place to browse, preview, and manage every document, attachment, and file associated with that workspace.

For teams managing dozens of spaces with hundreds of files, this eliminates the friction of context-switching and makes your workspace feel immediately navigable. Finding the right document now takes one click instead of three.
