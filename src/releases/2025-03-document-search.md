title: Document Search
date: 2025-03-11
tag: Tools
---

Interloom now automatically ingests and indexes every document in your workspace — files, notes, and attachments — making them searchable by your AI assistant. The platform processes uploaded documents in the background, breaking them into semantically meaningful chunks that the agent can retrieve when answering questions or working through a case.

When you ask the agent something, it doesn't just rely on the conversation thread — it searches across your entire document library to find the most relevant information, citing specific sources in its response.

For teams dealing with large volumes of reference material — policies, contracts, prior correspondence — this means the AI assistant has access to the same institutional knowledge your team does, without anyone needing to manually point it to the right file.
