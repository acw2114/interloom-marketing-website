---
title: Document Search
date: 2025-03-11
tag: Tools
---

Your AI assistant can now search across every document in your workspace — files, notes, and attachments. Documents are indexed automatically in the background, broken into semantically meaningful chunks the agent can retrieve when answering questions or working through a case.

When you ask the agent something, it searches your entire document library for the most relevant information and cites specific sources in its response. No one needs to manually point it to the right file.
