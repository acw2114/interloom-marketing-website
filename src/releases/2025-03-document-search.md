---
title: Document Search
date: 2025-03-11
tag: Tools
---

We now automatically ingest and index every document in your workspace — files, notes, and attachments — so the AI assistant can search across them. Documents are processed in the background, broken into semantically meaningful chunks the agent retrieves when answering questions or working through a case.

When you ask the agent something, it doesn't just rely on the conversation thread — it searches your entire document library for the most relevant information, citing specific sources in its response.

For teams dealing with large volumes of reference material — policies, contracts, prior correspondence — this means the assistant has access to the same institutional knowledge your team does, without anyone needing to manually point it to the right file.
