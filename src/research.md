<h2 class="text-2xl md:text-3xl font-serif mb-6">Context Graphs Are the New Essential Infrastructure for Agentic Systems</h2>
<p class="text-sm text-fg-4 mb-8">By Radu Miclaus, Tom Coshow, et al.</p>

<div class="grid grid-cols-1 md:grid-cols-2 gap-6 mb-8">
<div class="rounded-lg border border-line bg-surface-1" style="padding: 1.5rem;">
<p class="text-xs text-fg-4 font-mono tracking-wide uppercase mb-3">Key Finding</p>
<p class="text-fg-2 leading-relaxed mb-0">Context graphs track not only data context but also "decision flows" and event traces, enabling more informed and effective business decision making by AI agents.</p>
</div>
<div class="rounded-lg border border-line bg-surface-1" style="padding: 1.5rem;">
<p class="text-xs text-fg-4 font-mono tracking-wide uppercase mb-3">Strategic Planning Assumption</p>
<p class="text-fg-2 leading-relaxed mb-0">By 2028, over 50% of AI agent systems will leverage context graphs, driven by the need for increased domain specificity and operational grounding.</p>
</div>
</div>

<div class="rounded-lg border border-line bg-surface-1 mb-8" style="padding: 1.5rem;">
<p class="text-xs text-fg-4 font-mono tracking-wide uppercase mb-3">Recommendation</p>
<p class="text-fg-2 leading-relaxed mb-0">Extend context engineering beyond traditional vectorization and knowledge graphs by capturing "decision workflows" and business logic to deliver high-quality domain-specific agentic systems.</p>
</div>

<p class="text-fg-3 leading-relaxed mb-4">Gartner positions context graphs as the next evolution beyond knowledge graphs — capturing not just "what" and "who" through static entities, but "how" and "why" through decision traces and procedural logic that emerges from actual execution.</p>

<p class="text-fg-3 leading-relaxed mb-4">The report identifies five concrete benefits: capturing domain-specific organizational memory, differentiating in crowded AI markets, enabling compliance through "immutable audit trails," accelerating enterprise sales through clear ROI, and reducing implementation cost through domain-specific accelerators.</p>

<div class="rounded-lg bg-surface-1 mt-8" style="padding: 2rem; border-left: 3px solid var(--color-tint-blue);">
<h3 class="text-lg font-serif text-fg-1 mb-4">How Interloom Delivers on This</h3>

<div class="space-y-6">
<div>
<p class="text-sm font-medium text-fg-2 mb-1">Organizational Memory by Design</p>
<p class="text-sm text-fg-3 leading-relaxed mb-0">Interloom's <a href="/product/context-graph/" class="text-fg-1 no-underline hover:underline">context graph</a> is exactly the "persistent enterprise memory layer" Gartner describes. Every case an expert resolves becomes a precedent — a decision trace that captures not just what was decided, but the reasoning, context, and outcome.</p>
</div>

<div>
<p class="text-sm font-medium text-fg-2 mb-1">Knowledge That Emerges from Execution</p>
<p class="text-sm text-fg-3 leading-relaxed mb-0">Gartner emphasizes that context graphs should emerge from real operational data — emails, tickets, transcripts. Interloom does exactly this: it extracts operational expertise hidden in communication channels and transforms it into structured decision traces. The graph grows organically from how your team actually works.</p>
</div>

<div>
<p class="text-sm font-medium text-fg-2 mb-1">Compounding Decision Intelligence</p>
<p class="text-sm text-fg-3 leading-relaxed mb-0">Every resolved case adds institutional memory that becomes "increasingly hard to replace." Agents improve because the graph grows deeper — not because prompts are manually rewritten. Over time, this creates a durable competitive moat for every customer.</p>
</div>

<div>
<p class="text-sm font-medium text-fg-2 mb-1">Decision Lineage and Audit Trails</p>
<p class="text-sm text-fg-3 leading-relaxed mb-0">Every agent action traces back to the operational precedent that grounded it — providing the decision lineage, auditability, and governance that regulated industries require.</p>
</div>
</div>

<h2 class="text-2xl md:text-3xl font-serif mb-6">Demystifying Agent Operations in the Evolving AI Engineering Landscape</h2>
<p class="text-sm text-fg-4 mb-8">By Haritha Khandabattu, Arun Chandrasekaran, et al.</p>

<div class="grid grid-cols-1 md:grid-cols-2 gap-6 mb-8">
<div class="rounded-lg border border-line bg-surface-1" style="padding: 1.5rem;">
<p class="text-xs text-fg-4 font-mono tracking-wide uppercase mb-3">Core Argument</p>
<p class="text-fg-2 leading-relaxed mb-0">Most organizations "overinvest in agent construction and underinvest in agent operations." The result: agents that work in demos but fail at scale.</p>
</div>
<div class="rounded-lg border border-line bg-surface-1" style="padding: 1.5rem;">
<p class="text-xs text-fg-4 font-mono tracking-wide uppercase mb-3">Key Insight</p>
<p class="text-fg-2 leading-relaxed mb-0">"Trust in agents is no longer established at deployment. It must be continuously earned through evaluation, traceability, and the ability to intervene."</p>
</div>
</div>

<p class="text-fg-3 leading-relaxed mb-4">The primary risk is not catastrophic failure but "silent degradation" — agents that continue functioning while subtly drifting from expected behavior. Gartner's prescription: "progressively expand agent autonomy" based on production evidence, not upfront assumptions.</p>

<div class="rounded-lg bg-surface-1 mt-8" style="padding: 2rem; border-left: 3px solid var(--color-tint-blue);">
<h3 class="text-lg font-serif text-fg-1 mb-4">How Interloom Delivers on This</h3>

<div>
<p class="text-sm font-medium text-fg-2 mb-1">Trust Built into the Architecture</p>
<p class="text-sm text-fg-3 leading-relaxed mb-0">Agents can only act on what the context graph has grounded. They do not improvise from generic training data — they execute based on operational precedents that experts have validated.</p>
</div>

<div>
<p class="text-sm font-medium text-fg-2 mb-1">Progressive Autonomy through Expert Feedback</p>
<p class="text-sm text-fg-3 leading-relaxed mb-0">As experts resolve more cases, the context graph deepens, and agents gain more precedents. Autonomy expands organically because the grounding improves. When the graph lacks precedent, the agent escalates rather than guessing.</p>
</div>

<div>
<p class="text-sm font-medium text-fg-2 mb-1">Observability across Every Decision</p>
<p class="text-sm text-fg-3 leading-relaxed mb-0">Every agent action traces to a specific precedent in the context graph. If behavior changes, it is because the underlying operational knowledge changed — and that change is visible, auditable, and attributable.</p>
</div>
